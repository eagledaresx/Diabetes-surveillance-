⚠️ Mandatory Google Play Medical & Health Apps Disclaimer

Not a Medical Device • External Glucometer / CGM Required

Diabetes Care & Tracker (com.diabetescare.surveillance.twa) is an informational self-monitoring, logging, and educational reference application. It is not an FDA-cleared or CE-marked medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.

External Hardware Disclosure: This smartphone/tablet application cannot measure blood glucose, blood pressure, or blood oxygen (SpO2) using phone hardware, screens, or cameras alone. All biometric values must be entered manually by the user from a certified external blood glucose meter (glucometer) or synced from compatible external Continuous Glucose Monitoring (CGM) hardware (e.g., Dexcom, Abbott FreeStyle Libre, Accu-Chek, Contour Next). Always consult a licensed physician or endocrinologist before making any medical decisions or adjusting insulin/medication doses.

Privacy Policy & Data Safety

Privacy Policy

Effective & Last Updated: November 8, 2026

1. Developer & Data Controller Contact

Application: Diabetes Care & Tracker (com.diabetescare.surveillance.twa)
Developer Contact: Eagle Dares Development Team — eagledares@gmail.com

2. Data Collection, Encryption & Storage

  • On-Device AES-256-GCM Encryption: Blood glucose readings, fasting timers, meal logs, and medication reminders are encrypted on your device using WebCrypto AES-256-GCM with PBKDF2 key derivation and SHA-256 integrity checksums.
  • Zero Advertising or Data Selling: We do not sell, rent, or share your personal or medical data with third-party advertisers, data brokers, or analytics networks.
  • 100% Free Access: The application is completely free with no hidden subscriptions or unauthorized billing flows.

3. Android Permissions

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used for optional cloud meal analysis, language localization, and wearable API synchronization.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS & VIBRATE: Used strictly for user-configured morning fasting blood sugar reminders and medication alerts.
  • No High-Risk Permissions: The application does not request background location, contacts, SMS, call logs, or microphone access.
