Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 3, 2026

Your health privacy is absolute. The Diabetes Surveillance App (the "Application") is engineered around a client-first security model. We design, process, and persist clinical measurements locally so that your metabolic data remains strictly under your exclusive ownership.

02. Types of Information We Collect and Process

The Application operates as a specialized local medical analyzer tool. We collect and process the following classifications of user information:

  • ✓
    Metabolic Glucose Measurements & Fasting Timers Your logged fasting and post-meal blood sugar levels (mg/dL), dates, times, breakfast rules, and timer schedules.
  • ✓
    Medication Intake Logs & Active Prescriptions Prescription drug titles (e.g., Metformin, Insulin details) and daily taken confirmation logs designed to monitor drug adherence.
  • ✓
    Daily Hydration and Water Metrics Water intake amounts, liquid units (ML/OZ), target logging history, and consecutive day completion streaks.
  • ✓
    Optional Wearable Integration Integration Credentials User-supplied developer keys or OAuth credentials for Spike API, Dexcom Developer Platform, and Glooko Enterprise Servers, used purely as secure authentication proxies to stream continuous metrics.

03. Real-Time Data Storage & On-Device Boundaries

We do not store your medical data on external corporate servers.

  • •

    All blood sugar readings, hydration logs, and medication records are saved directly in your device's local sandbox storage (LocalStorage and isolated package directories).

  • •

    Uninstalling the Application securely wipes all corresponding medical files from your device immediately with no lingering traces.

04. Generative AI Diagnostics (Gemini API Integration)

Certain features of the app use high-fidelity, server-side Google Gemini models to analyze medication guides, customize traditional cultural recipes to the user's diabetic targets, and generate glycemic surveillance reports.

Prompt Transparency Rule: When generating these automated summaries, only raw metric logs (such as "Fasting Sugar: 121 mg/dL") and anonymized profile metrics are transmitted to the secure Google Gemini service. Personally identifiable attributes (such as names or physical addresses) are never included in these prompts. Google Gemini API keys are hosted in secured environment secrets and never rendered to public client inspection.

05. Android Permissions Disclosures

In compliance with Google Play Developer Policy requirements:

  • ℹ
    Camera & Files Storage: Optional permissions requested solely to capture physical glucose monitors or log photos locally at user command.
  • ℹ
    Background Location or Telephony Logs: Not requested. The software does not track any background positioning indices, phone statuses, or active IMEI accounts.

06. Security and HIPAA Alignment Disclaimer

Since data does not move through external database clouds managed by the Developer, the Developer is not classified as a "Covered Entity" under HIPAA regulations. However, the client code adheres to standard HIPAA security guidelines, utilizing encrypted browser environments and strict local process boundaries.

07. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to modify this policy as required by changing regulatory conditions. Whenever modifications are made, we will immediately update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy page and include corresponding app notices.

Medical Disclaimer Note

Diabetes Surveillance App delivers algorithmic tracking metrics and generic guidelines based on user inputs. It does not replace formal medical consultations, treatment orders, or diagnostic schedules. Always prioritize advice from certified medical practitioners.
